# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, empathetic and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on the work, never the person.
- Respect privacy and differing experience levels.
- Take responsibility, apologize and repair harm when mistakes happen.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and other
conduct that creates an unsafe environment are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `conduct@neotamia.re`. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
